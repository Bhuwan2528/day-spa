Advanced Plasma Fibroblast Treatment

A refined, non-surgical approach to skin tightening and rejuvenation. Plasma Fibroblast stimulates collagen production to restore firmness, improve texture and visibly lift the skin.

By activating the body’s natural healing response, this treatment gradually rebuilds the skin’s structure from within — delivering smoother, tighter and more youthful results without invasive procedures.

Multiple specialised treatments are available depending on the area being treated. Each service below is tailored for precision and optimal results.

Lip & Perioral Refinement

Targeted Treatment Area

The lip and surrounding area is one of the earliest regions to show fine lines and loss of elasticity. Plasma Fibroblast precisely targets this delicate zone, helping to smooth vertical lines, subtly lift the upper lip and refine the overall contour.

Unlike temporary solutions, this treatment works beneath the surface by stimulating collagen regeneration. The result is a natural, long-lasting improvement in both texture and firmness — without the need for injectables.
